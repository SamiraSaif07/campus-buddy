# Campus Buddy (ASU Matcher) — plan

A mobile-first web app that helps shy and first-year students at Applied Science Private University find a study or hangout buddy. Students sign in with an `@students.asu.edu.jo` email, set their free lecture blocks, and swipe through up to 15 profile cards a day, ordered by schedule overlap. Contact details stay hidden until both students swipe right; then the app hands them off to WhatsApp. There is no in-app chat. Solo build, 8 weeks, zero budget, live demo on Demo Night.
