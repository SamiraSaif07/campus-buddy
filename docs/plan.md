# Campus Buddy (ASU Matcher) — plan

A mobile-first web app that helps shy and first-year students at Applied Science Private University find a study or hangout buddy, strictly platonic. Students sign in with an `@students.asu.edu.jo` email, set their free lecture blocks, and swipe through up to 15 profile cards a day, ordered by schedule overlap and matched with their own gender by default. Each card flips in place to show extra photos and answers to curated campus prompts. Contact details stay hidden until both students swipe right; then the app hands them off to WhatsApp, and every match stays on a Buddies screen. There is no in-app chat. Solo build, 8 weeks, zero budget, live demo on Demo Night.
