# Three Candidate Problems

### 1. The Campus Social Isolation Gap at ASU (Selected)
* **Who has it:** New, shy, or freshman students at Applied Science University (ASU) who feel overwhelmed by the large student population, or don't have friends in an unfamilair environment.
* **How they cope today:** They look around lecture halls hoping to catch someone's eye, join massive, unorganized 500-member course WhatsApp groups, or simply isolate themselves on campus during gaps.

### 2. Manual Payment Status Confusion for Freelancers
* **Who has it:** Independent freelancers or student developers managing side contracts who struggle to track client payment cycles.
* **How they cope today:** They manually track statuses (paid, part-paid, overdue) in generic text notes apps and send awkward, manually drafted payment reminder messages over messaging apps.

### 3. Chaotic Campus Lost-and-Found Management
* **Who has it:** University students who lose personal items (like keys or student IDs) or find misplaced belongings on a crowded campus.
* **How they cope today:** They post random photos of found items to wide-reaching university social media groups, which accidentally exposes personal details or ownership tags to anyone online.
